The lifecycle

One pipeline, four ends.

Every feature hangs off the canonical stages. Scoring is yours to define — each user builds their own weighted scheme, and scores are versioned like everything else: how your judgement changed is itself provenance.

An idea can loop back at any point — it never loses its history.

  1. Capture Seconds, any format, from anywhere.
  2. Score Your own criteria and weights, tracked over time.
  3. Triage Decide what deserves attention now, later, or never.
  4. Develop Research and experiments, connected to your knowledge base.
  5. Decide Every developed idea reaches one of four ends —
  • Build Graduate to execution PM.
  • License / sell Publish to the marketplace.
  • Park Archive it; come back when it's time.
  • Legacy Seal it for succession.

Trust & provenance

Pre-patent IP demands promises.

Local and encrypted

The database is encrypted at rest with keys you set. Your machine is the vault and the source of truth — nothing leaves without an explicit action, and anything that does is encrypted to your keys first.

Full provenance

Who had what idea, when. Version history, activity log, authorship signals — the story of an idea is reconstructable end to end.

Agents on a leash

Agents are local to the app and act only through scoped, revocable tokens. Anything outward-facing — email, publish, sell — requires your approval.

No lock-in

Everything exports to open formats at any time. Required for legacy, honest everywhere else.

Working the ideas

Help that never leaks.

A local agent

No third-party LLM ever sees your ideas. The agent lives in the app with the whole context, and helps organically: shaping, scoring, organizing, enriching — including web search that discloses nothing of yours.

In the IP platform it becomes an expert IP and corporate lawyer. Every contribution is attributed and logged; human and agent authorship are always distinguishable.

A knowledge base as evidence

Add anything — papers, references, your own research — and connect it to ideas as support, prior art, contradiction, or inspiration.

An idea with a KB trail is a developed idea. The trail is part of its provenance, and part of its sale value.

Beyond the vault

Sell it, or pass it on.

Marketplace

You decide an idea is up for grabs. Buyers and bidders meet it on a separate, company-hosted webapp that only ever holds the curated face you chose to publish — pitch and selected artifacts. The vault stays private.

Sale, royalties, NDAs, escrow, and valuation are all handled mechanics.

Legacy

Legacy here means succession, not sentiment: your ideas and research are an intellectual estate. Because everything carries provenance, what you pass on is not a folder of files but an attributed, dated body of thought — who conceived what, when, and the evidence behind it. Heirs inherit the proof along with the work.

Designate who receives what: everything to one successor, or partitioned — these ideas to one person, that research to another — each partition encrypted with its own key. The encrypted database is delivered by the noder; the key travels by a separate, untraceable channel. Whoever holds both holds the rights.

Defensive publication as prior art is in scope, through the IP platform.

Ecosystem

A vault with satellites.

Idea Foundry owns the data. Everything around it exists to capture, carry, and back up — without ever being able to read what it holds.

  • ideafoundry This app — the source of truth, the vault, the workbench.
  • zephyrus coming soon Mobile capture; encrypts media and sends it to your paired instance.
  • oracle coming soon Encrypted store-and-forward; holds captures until your instance is back online.
  • noder coming soon Resilience — encrypted 3-2-1 backups, and the hand that executes legacy transitions.
  • relay coming soon Carries collaboration traffic between parties; run your own or use a hosted one.
  • marketplace coming soon Company-hosted webapp where buyers meet the ideas you published.

Satellites capture, relay, and back up — always ciphertext, never plaintext.

Non-goals

Not a social network. Sharing is deliberate, scoped, and per idea.

Not a cloud service. No central server ever holds plaintext.

Run it yourself

Yours by design.

Clone and run it on a local machine or private server behind your own access control. The project vision targets MIT licensing; a formal redistribution license is not yet included in the repository.
