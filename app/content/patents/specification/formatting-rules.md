Formatting for the specification, claims, and abstract is set by 37 CFR 1.52:

- Text on a 21.6 x 27.9 cm (letter) or A4 sheet, one side only.
- Text lines 1.5 or double spaced, with a legible, non-script font; no less than 12-point is the usual practice.
- Pages numbered consecutively; paragraph numbering ([0001]) is customary and helpful.
- Claims begin on a separate sheet; the abstract on its own sheet.
- Margins: the rule sets minimums for top, left, right, and bottom; source figures differ, so check the current rule text.
- Permissible filing: DOCX through Patent Center is accepted; otherwise PDF. Check the USPTO's current filing requirements.

Drawings have their own rules (37 CFR 1.84), including a different set of margins and sheet numbering.

Example: "[0014] FIG. 2 shows a flowchart of the verification method."

Correction: older sources referenced legacy tools (EFS-Web, EAST) and India ink drawings; current filing is through Patent Center and digital line drawings are accepted.

Tip: format with a template and run a final check against the official sample before filing.
