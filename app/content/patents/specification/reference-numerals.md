Reference numerals are the numbers on drawings that label each part (e.g. "processor 102") and are used in the description to point to it.

Rules (37 CFR 1.84(p) and MPEP 608.02):
- The same part must use the same numeral across all figures; different parts must use different numerals.
- Numerals mentioned in the description must appear in the drawings and vice versa.
- Numerals must be plain, legible, at least 0.32 cm high; not in brackets or circled except for leader conventions.
- Use leader lines (not arrows) to connect numerals to parts, without crossing.

Drafting practice: number by figure (100s in FIG. 1, 200s in FIG. 2) to ease reading, and avoid using numerals as claim limits (claims may include them in parentheses, and they do not limit the claim).

Example: "The mobile device 100 includes a camera 102 and a processor 104."

Tip: before filing, run a cross-check of numerals in the text against the drawings.
