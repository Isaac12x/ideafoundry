37 CFR 1.77 sets the preferred order of a utility application:

1. Title of the invention
2. Cross-reference to related applications
3. Statement on federally sponsored research (if any)
4. Parties to a joint research agreement (if any)
5. Sequence listing reference (if any)
6. Background
7. Brief summary
8. Brief description of the drawings
9. Detailed description
10. Claims
11. Abstract

The USPTO template in the source follows this order. Correction: when describing the field of invention, do not rely on old "U.S. patent classification definitions"; the USPC is retired and the USPTO now uses the CPC (Cooperative Patent Classification).

Example: Detailed description paragraphs numbered [0001], [0002]... with the claims starting on their own page.

Tips: headings are a preferred arrangement, not a fatal requirement; keep the claims and abstract each on a separate sheet; and check cross-reference wording (priority claims also go in the application data sheet).
