Free tools cover most first-pass searching.

- **Patent Public Search (USPTO)**: US grants and published applications, plus some foreign documents; Boolean and field searching. Replaced PatFT/AppFT and the older EAST/WEST tools.
- **Patent Center (USPTO)**: filing system and file wrappers (prosecution histories); replaced PAIR.
- **Global Dossier (USPTO)**: file histories and family members across participating offices.
- **Google Patents**: friendly full-text search, many countries, links to scholarly citations.
- **Espacenet (EPO)**: worldwide families and strong CPC browsing.
- **The Lens**: patents plus scholarly works, with exportable sets.
- **Non-patent sources**: Google Scholar, IEEE Xplore, ACM, arXiv, standards bodies, product manuals.
- **Paid tools** (e.g. Derwent, PatSnap): analytics, translation, and curated families; optional.

Tip: databases differ in coverage and date accuracy, so cross-check the publication date of anything you rely on.

Example (illustrative): find a candidate in Google Patents, confirm the family in Espacenet, then read its prosecution history in Patent Center to see what the examiner already said.

PTAB decisions are a separate public source for how references were argued.
