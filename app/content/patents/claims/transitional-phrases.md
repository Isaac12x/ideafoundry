The transition sets whether extra, unrecited elements can still infringe.

- **comprising** (also "including", "containing", "characterized by"): open. Adding more elements does not avoid infringement. Default for most claims.
- **consisting of**: closed. Excludes any element not listed; usually narrow. Avoid unless the invention truly needs it, or in a provisional where the wording may later limit you.
- **consisting essentially of**: partly closed. Allows extras that do not materially affect the basic and novel characteristics; mostly used in chemistry and compositions.

Example: a claim "A kit comprising a pen and a cap" covers a kit with a pen, cap, and case. "A kit consisting of a pen and a cap" does not cover the kit with a case.

Tip: in the preamble, "comprising" is the safe default; choosing "consisting of" trades coverage for clarity. The USPTO treats open and closed transitions per MPEP 2111.03.
