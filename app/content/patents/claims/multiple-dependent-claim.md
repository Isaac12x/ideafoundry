A multiple dependent claim refers to more than one earlier claim in the alternative ("The method of claim 1 or 2, wherein..."). It is allowed under 35 USC 112(e) and 37 CFR 1.75(c), but with limits:

- The references must be in the alternative ("any one of claims 1-3", "claim 1 or 2"), not cumulative ("claims 1 and 2").
- A multiple dependent claim may not depend on another multiple dependent claim.
- It counts as several claims for fee purposes (one per claim it could depend on); check the USPTO fee schedule for current surcharges.
- Each alternative path must itself satisfy the dependency (further-limiting) test.

Example: "The system of claim 1 or claim 2, wherein the database is local." Equivalent to two dependents in one sentence.

Tip: they save length but add fees and examiner objections, and a rejection of one path can taint the claim. Many US drafters use ordinary single-dependent claims; they are more common in EP/PCT-style sets.
