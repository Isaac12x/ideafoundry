A provisional is not upgraded. You file a new non-provisional application that claims the benefit of the provisional (35 USC 119(e)).

Requirements:
- File within 12 months of the provisional (14 months with a restoration petition showing unintentional delay).
- Name at least one common inventor.
- Put the benefit claim in the application data sheet: provisional number, filing date. A sentence in the specification does not do this.
- Present the claim by the later of 4 months from the non-provisional filing or 16 months from the provisional date, or petition to accept a late claim.

What keeps the date: only subject matter the provisional disclosed, explicitly or inherently, with enough detail to support the claim. New examples or limitations get the later date, and claims that need them are not entitled to the provisional date.

Steps in brief: review provisional; draft formal claims; reorganize the text into formal sections; make formal drawings; prepare the data sheet and inventor declarations; file; check the filing receipt shows the benefit claim.

Incorporation by reference of the provisional is a separate, helpful safeguard (it can rescue inadvertently omitted material under 37 CFR 1.57), but it requires a proper benefit claim on the filing date.

Example: provisional filed 3 Mar 2026; data sheet claims it; the new application is filed by 3 Mar 2027.
