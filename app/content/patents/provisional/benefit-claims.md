A benefit claim asks the USPTO to treat a later application as filed on an earlier date.

Kinds:
- Provisional: 35 USC 119(e). Later application within 12 months (14 with restoration).
- Continuation, divisional, CIP: 35 USC 120/121/365(c). Needs copendency with the parent and a chain of benefit claims to the first application.
- Foreign priority: 35 USC 119(a)-(d). US filing within 12 months of the foreign filing (6 for designs).

How: for applications filed on or after 16 Sept 2012, the reference (application number, filing date, relationship) must be in the application data sheet. The specification can repeat it, but that alone does not count.

Timing: present the claim within the later of 4 months from the actual filing date or 16 months from the earlier application's filing date. Later requires a petition showing the delay was unintentional.

Limits: the benefit extends only to claims the earlier application supports. Each application in a chain must be properly linked.

Check the filing receipt for the claim. A missing or wrong number is common and fixable, but only within the windows.

Example: non-provisional claims a provisional filed 5 Jan; data sheet lists "Provisional", its number and 5 Jan date.
