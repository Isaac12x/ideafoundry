Provisional: cheap, fast, unexamined, 12-month clock. Non-provisional (utility): formal claims, examined, can issue.

Choose a provisional when:
- The concept is early and you need a fast, defensible date (demo, investor meeting, preprint coming).
- You want 12 months to test the market or refine the claims.
- You plan foreign filing and want to start the 12-month priority period.

Choose a direct non-provisional when:
- Claims are ready and you want examination started now (including track-one style acceleration).
- You prefer not to spend an extra year before examination. A provisional does not shorten the 20-year term, which runs from the non-provisional filing date.

Traps:
- A provisional gives a date only for what it enables. Disclose thoroughly.
- Public disclosure first: US law gives a one-year grace period (102(b)(1)), most other countries do not. File before disclosing if foreign rights matter.
- A provisional filed within the US grace year still counts as the effective filing date if it supports the claims.
- Missing the 12-month point loses the benefit; restoration to 14 months is a narrow rescue.

Example: demo in six weeks, claims unsettled: file a detailed provisional now, then a non-provisional within 12 months.
