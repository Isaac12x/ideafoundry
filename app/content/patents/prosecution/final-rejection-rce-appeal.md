A second action that makes the rejection final ends routine back-and-forth. You can still reply, but amendments after final are limited: new limitations needing fresh search or consideration may be refused entry.

Options:
- **Request for Continued Examination (RCE)**: reopens prosecution so your amendment or new evidence is considered. Fees apply (see USPTO page).
- **Appeal to the Patent Trial and Appeal Board (PTAB)**: notice of appeal, appeal brief; the Board reviews the rejections. Further review is available at the Federal Circuit or by civil action.
- **Continuation**: abandon the application after filing a continuation so you can keep pursuing claims, possibly with new angles.
- **Interview and after-final reply**: sometimes enough to settle the issue.
- Accept allowable claims, pursue others in a continuation.

Timing: the reply period still runs; extensions are limited and fee-bearing.

Example (illustrative): claim 1 is finally rejected under 103. You file an RCE with an amendment adding the pre-launch timing and a declaration with test data.

Choose appeal when you believe the examiner's reasoning is legally wrong and arguments will not change.
